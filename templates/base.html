Navigating in Profile page

Steps to edit your profile:
  1. Click the ☰ menu icon at the top right of any page.
  2. Select Profile from the sidebar.
  3. Click the Edit Profile button on the left panel.
  4. Enter your bio (up to 30 characters).
  5. To change your avatar, click Choose File and select an image.
  6. Preview the image — you can change it again if needed.
  7. Click Save to apply your new profile details.
